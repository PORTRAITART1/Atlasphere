# Test Atlasphere in 5 Steps

**Welcome, Pioneer!**

Atlasphere is a decentralized governance and crowdfunding platform built on Pi Network. This guide will walk you through testing the app in 5 simple steps.

**Important:** Testing is voluntary. All transactions on Testnet use Test-Pi (no real value). Your feedback helps us build a safer, better platform.

---

## Step 1 — Open Atlasphere in Pi Browser

Open the **Pi Browser** app on your phone.

In the address bar, type:
atlasphere.pub.atoms.world

text

Wait for the app to load.

✅ **You should see:** The Atlasphere homepage with the slogan "Govern. Fund. Build."

---

## Step 2 — Sign in with Pi Network

Tap the **"Connect Pi"** button (top-right corner).

A Pi authentication window will appear.

Tap **"Authorize"** to grant access to:
- Your Pi username
- Payment permissions (for testing)

✅ **You should see:** Your Pi username appear in the top-right corner.

---

## Step 3 — Explore and Test

Choose **one** of these tests:

### Option A — Vote on a Proposal
1. Tap **"Proposals"** in the menu
2. Open any active proposal
3. Tap **"Vote For"** or **"Vote Against"**
4. Confirm your vote

### Option B — Make a Test Contribution
1. Open any proposal
2. Enter an amount (e.g., 1 Test-Pi)
3. Tap **"Pay with Pi Network"**
4. Authorize the payment in Pi Browser

✅ **You should see:** A confirmation message ("Success" or "Vote recorded").

---

## Step 4 — Verify the Confirmation

After your action, check that:

- ✅ The vote/contribution appears in the proposal page
- ✅ Your reputation score may have increased
- ✅ The transaction is visible in **"Dashboard"** or **"My Activity"**

If something doesn't work, note the exact error message.

---

## Step 5 — Share Your Feedback

We need your honest feedback to improve.

**Option A — In-app feedback**
1. Tap the **💬 feedback button** (bottom-right)
2. Rate your experience (1-5 stars)
3. Describe any issue or suggestion
4. Submit

**Option B — Direct message**
Send us a message on:
- **Pi Chat:** @portraitart

✅ **You should see:** A "Thank you" confirmation.

---

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| App won't load | Close Pi Browser completely and reopen |
| "Connect Pi" doesn't work | Make sure you're in Pi Browser (not Chrome) |
| Payment window doesn't open | Check that you granted "payments" scope |
| Vote not recorded | Refresh the page and check "My Activity" |
| Error message appears | Take a screenshot and send it to us |
