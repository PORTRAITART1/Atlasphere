Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Atlasphere is committed to protecting the privacy of users of its decentralized governance platform on Pi Network.

2. Data Collected

We may collect your Pi Network UID and username, voting history, proposals, contributions, quest progress, reputation score, language preferences, and interface settings.

3. Use of Data

Data is used to authenticate users, manage governance activity and contributions, calculate reputation, track quests, and improve the platform.

4. Pi Transactions

Pi transactions are processed through the official Pi Network SDK. We record payment amounts and statuses for community-funding transparency and never store private keys.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Votes and contributions may be publicly visible for governance transparency. Pi identifiers may be shared with Pi Network for authentication.

6. Security

We use reasonable security measures, including encryption in transit and secure Pi Network authentication.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable law.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Atlasphere uses local storage for language preferences and authentication sessions. No third-party tracking cookies are used by this page.

9. Contact

Contact Atlasphere through the Pi Network platform or the community support channel for privacy questions.
