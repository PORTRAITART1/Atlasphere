Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Atlasphere ("we", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users ("you"). This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our decentralized governance platform on Pi Network.

2. Data Collected

We collect the following data:

  • Pi Network identifier (UID) and username during authentication
  • Voting history, proposals, and contributions on the platform
  • Quest progress data and reputation score
  • Language preferences and interface settings

3. Use of Data

Your data is used to:

  • Authenticate your identity via Pi Network SDK
  • Manage your votes, proposals, and financial contributions
  • Calculate your reputation score and voting weight
  • Track your progress in community quests
  • Improve the platform and personalize your experience

4. Pi Transactions

Pi transactions are processed via the official Pi Network SDK. We record payment amounts and statuses to ensure transparency of community funding. We never store your private keys.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Votes and contributions are publicly visible on the platform to ensure governance transparency. Your Pi identifier may be shared with Pi Network for authentication.

6. Security

We use reasonable security measures, including encryption in transit and secure Pi Network authentication.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable law.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Atlasphere uses local storage for language preferences and authentication sessions. No third-party tracking cookies are used by this page.

9. Contact

Contact Atlasphere through the Pi Network platform or the community support channel for privacy questions.
